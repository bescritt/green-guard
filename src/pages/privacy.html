Privacy Policy

SafeBrowsing+ classifies pages on your device. By default:

  • A local bloom filter pre-filters known-bad domains without sending anything off-device.
  • Heuristic signals are extracted from the page and evaluated locally.
  • The analytics driver (if enabled) receives only the serialisable feature summary — never the full page text — and only to improve classification.
  • No browsing history, passwords, or page content is sold or shared.

If you submit a manual report, the reported URL and report type are sent to the crowdsourced pipeline. You can disable report-sharing in Settings.

This extension stores its configuration and threat list in your browser's local storage. It does not use third-party analytics.
